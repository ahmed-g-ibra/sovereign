# 1. Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-10-21

## Status

Accepted

## Context

This project exists partly to demonstrate engineering judgement. Judgement that
leaves no trace is indistinguishable from luck, and six months from now neither
an interviewer nor I will remember why a choice was made.

## Decision

Every decision with a real alternative gets a short dated file here. Not every
decision — only the ones where someone could reasonably have chosen otherwise.

## Consequences

When asked "why Postgres and not Timestream?", the answer is a document written
at the time with the numbers that drove it, rather than a reconstruction.
