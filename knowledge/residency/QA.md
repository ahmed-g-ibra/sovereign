Qatar was the first GCC state with a general data protection law, in force
since 2017. The Qatar Financial Centre operates a separate regime for entities
established within it.

This entry is the thinnest in the set and should be treated as a placeholder
until someone reads the law and the current ministry guidelines.

**Relevance to this project:** Google's Doha region (`me-central1`) is
measurable and performs well from the Gulf, making Qatar a credible target for
regional workloads where the legal position allows it.
