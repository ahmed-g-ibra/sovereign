The UAE has three overlapping regimes: the federal PDPL, and separate laws in the DIFC and ADGM financial free zones. Which applies depends on where the entity is established, not where the data sits, and getting that wrong is the most common mistake in UAE cloud architecture.

The federal law permits cross-border transfer subject to conditions. The sharper constraint for cloud selection is sectoral: health data and certain financial data are reported to carry in-country requirements that the general law does not impose. Those sector rules, not the PDPL, are usually what forces a UAE region.

Relevance to this project: the UAE is the best-connected measurement target in the Gulf, with AWS me-central-1, Azure UAE North and Central, and two Oracle regions. For an Omani workload it is the nearest in-region option and the default answer absent a reason to go elsewhere.
