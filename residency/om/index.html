🇴🇲 Oman

Oman has a general personal data protection law in force since 13 February 2023, with Executive Regulations issued in 2024. Reporting indicates the transition period ended in early 2026, making the regime fully enforceable.

For cloud region selection the practical question is whether personal data may leave Oman and under what conditions. The law permits cross-border transfer subject to conditions rather than prohibiting it outright, but the specific conditions must be read from the Executive Regulations before anyone relies on this entry.

Relevance to this project: Oman has no measurable in-country public cloud region. Oracle's Muscat presence is a Dedicated Region with no public endpoint (see ADR 0005), so Omani workloads necessarily land in UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia or Europe. That makes the cross-border conditions the deciding factor for anyone building here, not a footnote.
