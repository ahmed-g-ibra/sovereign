🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia

Draft — not verified. This entry was written from secondary sources and has not been checked against the instrument. Read the primary source before relying on anything here.

Last verifiednever

Saudi Arabia's PDPL has been enforceable since September 2024, with no general grace period remaining. SDAIA issued a dedicated regulation on transfers outside the Kingdom in 2024 and risk-assessment guidance in early 2025.

Transfers abroad are permitted where conditions and safeguards are met, and a controller relying on safeguards is expected to carry out a risk assessment first. SDAIA has signalled it will publish an adequacy list and review it periodically; whether that list exists yet, and who is on it, is an open question that materially changes the answer for any Saudi workload.

The law reaches processing of data about people in the Kingdom even when the processor sits abroad, so "we host in Frankfurt" does not remove the obligation.

Relevance to this project: two in-country regions are arriving at once — AWS targets December 2026 and Azure Saudi East is slated for Q4 2026 — joining Google's Dammam region. Saudi is about to go from one hyperscaler region to four, which is the single biggest change to Gulf cloud architecture in years.
