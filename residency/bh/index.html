🇧🇭 Bahrain

Bahrain's law is reported to take a more restrictive approach to cross-border transfer than its neighbours, with transfers to countries outside an approved list requiring authority approval. If accurate, that makes Bahrain the strictest GCC jurisdiction on this specific question and the one most likely to force an in-country or listed-country region.

That claim is exactly the kind this project must not publish on a draft basis. Verify before the site states it.

Relevance to this project: AWS me-south-1 sits in Bahrain and is the oldest hyperscaler region in the Gulf. Our first vantage point could not complete a TCP connection to its S3 endpoint, which is still unexplained and may be local to that probe.
